# Silkscreen firmware requirements

Derived from `de-link_pcb/docs/HARDWARE.md` (authoritative) and `de-link_pcb/DESIGN_REVIEW.md` (plus its `docs/final-review-2026-09-19/` sections) on 2026-09-29, then checked item-by-item by an independent verification pass against those documents (159 confirmed, 10 corrected, 1 unverifiable). Corrections are shown inline. Source paths are relative to the `de-link_pcb` repository.

Rows found wrong after the sleep-drain audit of 2026-09-30 are rewritten and marked **Corrected 2026-09-30**. The itemized sleep budget, the per-pin sleep states and the drain rules are in [POWER.md](POWER.md). How the firmware tells the board's variants apart (panel family, boot-time probes, the front light on use) is in [MODULARITY.md](MODULARITY.md).

Rows changed after the hardware audit of 2026-09-30 (Rev 1.01) carry a 2026-10-01 note. The audit found no mismatch between the firmware and the board; its bench procedure, `de-link_pcb/docs/audit-2026-09-30/BRING_UP.md`, is mapped onto the test firmware in [TEST-SUITE.md](TEST-SUITE.md#bench-bring-up-bring_upmd).

**Levels:** `must` = the board misbehaves or is damaged/drained without it; `should` = strongly recommended; `info` = context firmware needs.

## Additional items raised during verification

- USB with no cell fitted: P+ and therefore BAT_MONIT read about 0 V while USB_STAT reads the 3.3 V idle level (HARDWARE.md:439-440, 501-503). The low-battery shutdown (item 65) and the Wi-Fi gate (item 67) must apply only while the mux is on the cell (USB_STAT on-battery or weak-USB window). Otherwise a USB-powered board with no battery deep-sleeps or boot-loops, or never gets Wi-Fi. **Firmware (2026-10-01):** the cutoff never applies on external power or to a reading below 2.5 V, and on external power with P+ below 2.5 V, or while USB_STAT shows the charger's no-battery blink, the header and the reader's status bar say "No battery" in place of a charge state (`PowerConfig::noChargeableCellNotice`).
- The on-battery USB_STAT level (2.20 V) is unverified. If the TP4056 status pins clamp to VCC, the reading could shift toward the 1350-1800 'unknown' band. The TP4056 CE pin driven by Q2 might also back-feed USB_VBUS on battery (D2 lit, sleep current about 7x). Bench-measure USB_STAT, USB_VBUS and sleep current on battery before freezing the windows (03_ldo_power_tree_sleep_budget.md:456, 468, 471; DESIGN_REVIEW.md:448). **2026-10-01:** the test firmware's T2 stage 1 judges the battery-only level (BRING_UP section 4): a sample at or below 1350 mV (the charging or complete band) fails `hw08_clamp`; one in 1350-2100 mV warns `partial_clamp`, 2300-2600 mV `above_nominal`, and above 2600 mV `st_not_asserting` (ST not asserting). If real boards fail it, the P+ trend check in [POWER.md HW-08](POWER.md#6-hardware-notes-for-a-future-revision) is the firmware contingency.
- Pin release re-enables pulls: per 03:469 (PWR-19), gpio_reset_pin(), which drivers call when they release a pin, leaves a ~45 k pull-up enabled. After any SDMMC, LEDC or SPI deinit, re-configure every board pin explicitly. On GPIO42 that pull-up asserts ADIM (3.07 V > 1.2 V, so the frontlight turns on, possibly into an open load and an OVP latch). On the SD bus it back-feeds a gated card. On GPIO18 it reads about 2.27 V. On GPIO40 it selects warm. On the ADC pins it shifts the readings.
- Upstream SDK hazards found in a read-only check of upstream-cp freeink-sdk: SdmmcBlockDevice.cpp:37 enables SDMMC_SLOT_FLAG_INTERNAL_PULLUP. Lines 88-92 power-cycle the gate (HIGH 80 ms, then LOW) with the slot already initialised and the bus not parked, so SD_VDD is back-fed and this is not a real power cycle. end() at lines 117-121 deinits without parking the bus or gating power off. Together these violate items 32-35.
- More upstream SDK hazards: PowerManager.cpp:61/71 and InputManager.cpp:96/110/131 put INPUT_PULLDOWN on an active-high power pin (violates item 57). BatteryMonitor.cpp:371 puts INPUT_PULLUP on the charge-status pin (violates item 80). EpdBus.cpp:108 gives BUSY no pull-down (item 21). FrontlightManager treats warm/cool as a second PWM channel (BoardConfig.h:579-585) and has no ADIM enable pulse or OVP-recovery sequence (items 99 and 108-109). platformio.ini has no brown-out setting (item 68).
- The line numbers in the two items above are from upstream freeink-sdk `87c4493`. Upstream `bbd528c`, the SDK pin since the `5b1f0605` [upstream merge](PORTING.md#upstream-merges) (`2339226` before it), leaves every one of these hazards as it was; `bbd528c` changes only ContentProtection and Opds. Metalio-only code added above some of them moves SdmmcBlockDevice.cpp by 23 lines, BoardConfig.h by 12 (the warm/cool block is at 591-597), EpdBus.cpp by 10 and InputManager.cpp by 3. The Silkscreen profile's fields avoid each SDK hazard ([PORTING.md, Architecture](PORTING.md#architecture-silkscreen-is-data)), and other boards keep upstream's behaviour. The brown-out level is still open (PORTING.md, "Not yet").
- Serialise loads: 03:452 (PWR-02) says never run Wi-Fi TX during an SD write or an EPD refresh (worst case 515 mA against the LDO's 560 mA minimum current limit). This is stricter than item 12's 'avoid long overlaps'.
- The power button needs software debounce. Its RC filter is only (10 k || 100 k) x 2.2 nF = 20 us (04_esp32_core_gpio_map.md:237-242). Item 48 covers only the ladders.
- The touch controller part and address are not specified in the hardware docs (HARDWARE.md:871-874). **Answered 2026-09-30:** the -FT01C drawing names an FT6336U; the probe accepts 0x38 only with its chip id (`touch-identity`), and the GT911 fallback is built only for the alternate jumpers (`touch-gt911-optin`). The old port assumed FT6336U at 0x38 with a GT911 fallback at 0x5D (old freeink-sdk BoardConfig.h:1829-1848). GT911 latches its address from the INT level during reset, so GPIO41 must be driven during the TP_RST sequence. Also confirm touch comes out on the J4 tail, not on J2 pins 6/7 (06:376, EPD-08).
- Firmware must set per-build flags for: frontlight fitted (and whether J3 or an external J6 string), touch fitted, U13 or U14 fitted, and the UP(2)-as-power variant (R72/R74). Several rules depend on these (never enable the boost unplugged, OVP trip level, dropping UP(2) from ladder 2). **Updated 2026-09-30:** touch and the RTC are probed (and identified, `touch-identity`, `rtc-identity`), and UP(2)-as-power needs nothing (`pwr-button-idle-check` catches a half-done rework). The front light needs no flag or declaration: it is compiled in, and every switch-on the user asks for probes for a load and for each string (`fl-load-probe`, `fl-missing-string`). One trip level (23 V) serves J3 and J6. Only the panel family is a build choice; the tiers are set out in [MODULARITY.md](MODULARITY.md).
- If GPIO1/GPIO4 are not armed as wake sources, set them analog (or apply an RTC hold) before deep sleep, so a held mid-rail key does not leave the digital input buffer in its linear region (08:456, HMI-06).
- The ladder window tolerances conflict between documents: HARDWARE.md:940 uses +/-1.5% rail and +/-50 mV, while 08:465/948 use a TLV75533 +/-2% rail, windows of about +/-3% of full scale (~93 mV), and a UP(2) maximum of 2942 mV. The proposed windows have about 0 mV margin at the BACK lower edge and miss the UP(1) lower corner by 3 mV. Calibrate on hardware and confirm the released level top-codes above the released threshold.
- Always-on 3V3 loads (the e-paper panel and the touch controller) are not measured (03:457, PWR-07). Panel deep sleep is 2-6 uA only after the deep-sleep command (06:491). Measure total sleep current against the ~60 uA budget.
- Do not flash the stock de-link build or board profile onto this board: the PSRAM type and every GPIO differ (04:283, MCU-13). Build a new BoardProfile and env.
- U14 (RV-8263-C7) is a conventional single-supply RTC with a different register map (HARDWARE.md:1087, 1102-1108). It needs its own driver and its own oscillator-stop handling. The DS3231M VBAT-only rules apply only to U13.
- Power-up glitches: GPIO1-14, GPIO17 and the XTAL_32K pins glitch LOW for 60 us, and GPIO18 glitches both LOW and HIGH for 60 us (04:74-93, 268-276; 05:329). This includes EPD CS/SCLK, and TP_RST gives the touch controller a reset pulse. All harmless, but firmware should still run full panel and touch resets and not trust peripheral state at cold boot.
- The hardware docs give no EPD SPI clock (the old port used 20 MHz). Choose it from the SSD1677 datasheet limit and bench-check it.
- Boot-loop and fast-sleep firmware can shut the USB-Serial-JTAG flashing window, and SW6 is DNP (04:282, 306-333). Add a firmware escape hatch (for example, stay awake at boot while a key is held or USB is inferred present). Item 89 covers only the USB-present case.

## Open questions

- Button decode windows are not given in HARDWARE.md or DESIGN_REVIEW.md. Only nominal levels and the rule 'ignore non-clean levels' are (HARDWARE.md:908-945). The windows in btn-windows-ladder1 and btn-windows-ladder2 are derived from +/-1% resistors, +/-1.5% rail and +/-50 mV ADC. BACK (2.80 V) and UP(2) (2.877 V, worst case up to 2.93 V) sit at or above the 2900 mV specified 11 dB range and in the compressed region (final-review 08:475). The margin between UP(2) and the saturated 'released' reading needs measuring on real hardware.
- HARDWARE.md:944 and DESIGN_REVIEW.md:454 say to reject readings not near 'one of the six clean levels of its ladder', but each ladder has only 5 clean levels (4 keys + idle). Should chords with the 100 Ohm key count as a sixth level, or is 'six' a typo?
- SD parking order: HARDWARE.md:1307-1311 says to drive the lines LOW and 'hold >= 20 ms' but does not say whether the 20 ms comes before or after SD_ACTIVATE goes HIGH. I read it as: lines LOW, then gate off, then keep LOW >= 20 ms. Final-review 05_sdmmc_microsd.md:274-280 (7.4 ms to reach 0.5 V with lines LOW) supports that reading.
- GPIO10 power-up glitch: HARDWARE.md:599-600 says IO10 has 'no power-up glitch', but final-review 05_sdmmc_microsd.md:329 cites ESP32-S3 DS Table 2-2 (a 60 us low glitch). The doc should be reconciled. Firmware assumes the glitch exists and power-cycles the card before the first mount.
- GPIO45 strap: HARDWARE.md:1169-1173 says GPIO45 HIGH at reset stops the module booting (1.8 V VDD_SPI). Final-review 04_esp32_core_gpio_map.md:285 says PSRAM modules fix VDD_SPI by eFuse so GPIO45 is ignored. Firmware leaves GPIO45 alone either way; the docs disagree.
- DS3231M in VBAT-only mode: confirm the EOSC bit behaviour (control register 0x0E bit 7), the OSF state after the first I2C access, and I2C VIH with VCC grounded (DESIGN_REVIEW.md:576 item f). The rtc-eosc-zero requirement comes from datasheet knowledge, not the board docs.
- The touch controller part number, I2C address, INT polarity/drive type and coordinate orientation for the -T01C/-FT01C panel are not in the hardware docs. The old port assumed FT6336U at 0x38 with a GT911 0x5D fallback, swapXY and flipY (silkscreen-firmware/freeink-sdk/libs/hardware/BoardConfig/include/BoardConfig.h, SILKSCREEN_TOUCH). **Partly answered 2026-09-30:** the part is the FT6336U named on the -FT01C drawing, at 0x38, identified by its chip id 0x64 (`touch-identity`); the GT911 is an opt-in fallback for the alternate jumpers (`touch-gt911-optin`). INT behaviour and orientation still wait for B9.
- The EPD SPI clock is not specified in the hardware docs. The old port used 20 MHz (old BoardConfig.h SILKSCREEN displaySpiHz); upstream DE_LINK uses 0 = SSD1677 default (40 MHz when this was written, upstream-cp/freeink-sdk/libs/hardware/BoardConfig/include/BoardConfig.h:1175; 20 MHz since upstream's `2372870` of 2026-10-11, and the shipped SILKSCREEN profile names 20 MHz itself). Validate with the 33 Ohm series resistors and the FPC.
- SDMMC clock: the design intent is about 40 MHz 4-bit (HARDWARE.md:568), while the old port's latest commits were testing 400 kHz on a DevKitC. Which default should the re-port ship (for example, the 20 MHz SDMMC_FREQ_DEFAULT) until first-article checks pass?
- USB-insert wake and awake policy: nothing can wake the chip in hardware on USB insertion (no VBUS GPIO; USB_STAT is analog). Options are to stay awake while USB is inferred present (the old port did this, commit c88d1cfb, silkscreen-firmware/src/main.cpp:456-457) or to use a periodic timer wake to poll USB_STAT. The trade-off is sleep current against flashing access and charge UI.
- Wake-source combination: can ext0 (GPIO18 HIGH) and ext1 ANY_LOW (GPIO1|GPIO4) be used together on ESP32-S3 with the pinned IDF (pioarduino 55.03.311)? What does keeping the RTC_PERIPH domain on for ext0 cost in sleep current? Alternatively, ext1 would need per-pin levels (HIGH for 18, LOW for 1/4). **Partly answered 2026-09-30:** RTC_PERIPH on costs about 1 uA (8 against 7 uA, ESP32-S3 DS v2.2 Table 5-10). The port uses ext1 ANY_HIGH on GPIO18 alone and has no ladder wake.
- The on-battery USB_STAT level assumes the unpowered TP4056 CHRG/STDBY pins are truly Hi-Z; their leakage is unspecified (DESIGN_REVIEW.md:448; final-review 03_ldo PWR-18). Measure the unplugged reading on a real lot to confirm it lands inside 1800-2600 mV. T2 stage 1 does (`hw08_clamp`, `partial_clamp`).
- No-battery detection with USB present: with Fix 4 holding CE low, what BAT_MONIT/P+ reads when no cell is fitted is not characterised. Is there a reliable 'no cell' signature besides USB_STAT reading a steady 3.3 V (which is the same as charger idle)? **Partly answered 2026-10-01:** P+ below 2.5 V (`BatteryMonitor::CELL_PRESENT_MIN_MV`) while USB_STAT shows external power, or the no-battery blink at any P+ (the charger then holds P+ near 4.2 V), means no cell, or one too flat for the charger to start, and the reader says so (`PowerConfig::noChargeableCellNotice`). T1 measures P+ on USB with no cell; what BAT_MONIT reads there is still to see on a board.
- Exact low-battery thresholds (percent curve, shutdown point, Wi-Fi gate) need choosing. The docs give only 3.0-4.2 V, '~3.3 V ~ 0%' (HARDWARE.md:423-424) and '>= ~3.4 V for Wi-Fi' (final-review PWR-04), and readings run low under load.
- The brown-out level change (PWR-04: level 3 = 2.98 V or level 4 = 2.84 V) needs a custom sdkconfig. Confirm that the upstream pioarduino Arduino build can set CONFIG_ESP_BROWNOUT_DET_LVL_SEL, or set the level at runtime.
- Frontlight open-load detection: J3 presence cannot be sensed before enabling, and LED_MONIT (10.7 ms RC) is slower than U10's OVP. Is the proposed about 22 V (about 2357 mV at GPIO2) software trip right, and how many automatic retries should follow a latch? **Answered 2026-09-30 (owner decision):** presence is sensed as part of the enable (`fl-load-probe`: 23 V trip, a reading every 20 ms for 500 ms since 2026-10-01). No automatic retry: the user switching on again is the retry, and Restore Light on Wake stays dark after a no-light result until a cold boot. The window length still wants a scope check at bring-up (B8).
- Colour change: holding ADIM at duty 0 for more than 2.5 ms shuts U10 down, and C9 keeps the previous string's Vf (it decays through 1.12 MOhm, tau about 5 s). The safe dwell and discharge strategy for switching to a lower-Vf string is unmeasured (DESIGN_REVIEW.md:415). **Partly answered 2026-09-30:** after a trip, COLOR_SEL waits for LED_SW at 15 V or less (`fl-charged-rail-wait`). A colour change between two lit strings still switches after 3 ms dark, so the lower-Vf case stays a bench item.
- Alternate power-button build (R72/R74 fitted, R36/R73 removed): does SW7 then drive PWR_BUTTON/GPIO18, and at what level? HARDWARE.md §9.2 gives the option and the order of the rework; the level follows from the netlist. **Answered 2026-10-01:** yes. With R36 and R73 removed and R72 and R74 fitted, SW7 takes 3V3 (through R74, 0 Ohm) to R72 10 kOhm into PWR_BUTTON, against R76's 100 kOhm pull-down: about 3.0 V pressed (3.3 V x 100k / 110k) and 0 V released, active HIGH, the same as SW10 through R62 (netlist; HARDWARE.md §9.2). The ext1 ANY_HIGH wake on GPIO18 is unchanged, and UP(2) is no longer on GPIO4: its ladder-2 window never fires. R36 must come off first, or GPIO18 idles at about 1.76 V (`pwr-button-idle-check`). The test firmware checks the build with OPT's "UP(2) is POWER" (T3, `up2_window`).
- Panel orientation, bezel/viewable insets, and page-turn semantics (which of UP/DOWN means next page) are not in the hardware docs. The old port used viewableInsets {9,3,3,3} and NO_FLIP. **Insets answered 2026-09-30:** every measured case window (4.26 Rev K/L and P03, 3.68, 5.76) is the active area plus 0.5-0.7 mm, so the profile uses {0,0,0,0}.
- Possible secondary USB-present cue, needing bench characterisation: with the frontlight off, LED_MONIT tracks LDO_IN minus a diode drop, so comparing it with BAT_MONIT (P+) might separate USB (LDO_IN about 5 V) from battery. The diode drop is not fixed (HARDWARE.md:846-851), so reliability is unknown.
- Old-port behaviour the re-port should not carry forward. (a) Stale USB_STAT windows: ST 1700-2250 and charging 850-1400 mV, based on pre-rescale 1.98 V (silkscreen-firmware/lib/hal/HalGPIO.cpp:258-266; freeink-sdk/libs/hardware/BatteryMonitor/src/BatteryMonitor.cpp:447-452, 529-531). (b) INPUT_PULLDOWN on GPIO18 (freeink-sdk/libs/hardware/InputManager/src/InputManager.cpp:124-127). (c) COLOR_SEL driven as an LEDC PWM blend (freeink-sdk/libs/hardware/FrontlightManager/src/FrontlightManager.cpp:336-343); since 2026-10-01 the re-port blends again, but only on two strings the probe has lit since the reset, off the carrier while dark. (d) No explicit >=100 us ADIM enable pulse was found in FrontlightManager (grep). (e) Internal SD pull-ups enabled on top of the external 10k (freeink-sdk/libs/hardware/SDCardManager/src/SdmmcBlockDevice.cpp:35, 43-45). (f) The ladder windows LEFT 1050-1350 and UP(1) 1650-1950 sit close to chord levels (InputManager.cpp:195-212).
